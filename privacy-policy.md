This privacy policy applies to the Kitchen Saathi app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Habuild Healthtech Pvt. Ltd. (collectively, the "Application"). Habuild Healthtech Pvt. Ltd., having its registered office at Bothra Complex, Model Mill Square, Ganeshpeth Colony, Nagpur, Maharashtra 440018, India, is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider" and is the data controller / data fiduciary for the personal data described in this policy.

**Effective date: 2026-07-23**

## Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

**Information you provide:**

- Mobile number (used to create and sign in to your account; a one-time password is sent to verify it)
- Name
- Health and wellness information you choose to share (see "Health and Wellness Data" below)
- Messages you send in the in-app chat, including voice messages you choose to record

**Information collected automatically:**

- Your device's Internet Protocol address
- The pages/screens of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages
- The time spent on the Application
- Your mobile operating system and version
- Device model and device identifiers
- Application version
- Network connection type (e.g., Wi-Fi or mobile data)
- In-app interaction events (e.g., screen views, button taps, feature usage) collected for analytics
- Crash reports and diagnostic information

## Health and Wellness Data

To personalise your experience, the Application may ask you to share health and wellness information, such as health conditions or concerns (for example: blood pressure, diabetes, thyroid, PCOD/PCOS). Conversations you have with the in-app assistant may also contain health-related information.

- This information is collected **only with your explicit consent**, which you give by selecting the relevant options or by choosing to share such information in the chat.
- It is used **solely** to personalise the content, recommendations, and responses provided to you within the Application.
- It is **never sold**, and it is **not used for advertising** or shared with advertisers.
- It is shared only with the service providers who operate the chat and personalisation features on the Service Provider's behalf (see "Third Party Access").
- You may withdraw consent or request deletion of this information at any time by contacting the Service Provider at saransh.dua@habuild.in.

## Voice Messages and Microphone

The Application can access your device's microphone **only** when you choose to record a voice message in the chat. Voice recordings are processed to understand your message and deliver a response, and are handled under the same protections as other chat and health data described in this policy. The microphone is not used for any other purpose.

## Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, software development kits (SDKs), local storage, secure on-device storage, session tokens, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, and service delivery. Specifically:

- **Secure device storage** is used to keep you signed in between sessions.
- **Cookies and local storage** may be used by in-app web content (such as the chat interface) to function correctly.
- **An analytics SDK (Mixpanel)** is used to understand how the Application is used, in pseudonymised form (see "Third Party Access").

The Application does **not** use advertising pixels (such as the Facebook/Meta Pixel) or third-party advertising trackers. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

## Legal Bases for Processing

Where the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or similar laws apply, the Service Provider processes personal data on the following legal bases:

- **Consent** — health and wellness data, voice messages, analytics where consent is required, and marketing communications.

- **Legitimate interests** — securing the Application, preventing fraud and abuse (including rate-limiting), and diagnosing technical problems.
- **Legal obligation** — retaining records where required by applicable law.
